Support & Ideas

Bayan Al-Qasas is currently in beta.

This app is created to help learners improve their Arabic through stories, audio narration, subtitles, and reading practice.

We would love to hear your suggestions, ideas, or any issues you find so we can improve the app.

Contact Support

For support, suggestions, story ideas, or problem reports, please contact us at:

bayanalqasas.support@gmail.com

When contacting us

To help us understand your request, you may include:

  • The issue or suggestion
  • Your device type
  • Your app version
  • Your selected language
  • Steps to reproduce the problem, if any
