Bayan Al-Qasas

Bayan Al-Qasas is an educational storytelling app designed to help learners improve their Arabic through stories, audio narration, subtitles, and reading practice.

About the App

The app provides high-quality Arabic stories in clear classical Arabic Fus’ha, with audio narration and multilingual subtitles to support Arabic learners.
