Privacy Policy

Language note: This Privacy Policy is currently provided in English. Arabic, Indonesian, and Malay translations may be added in future updates.

Effective Date: June 2026

Bayan Al-Qasas is an educational storytelling application designed to help learners improve their Arabic language skills through stories, audio narration, subtitles, and reading practice.

1. Information We Collect

Bayan Al-Qasas does not require users to create an account, and we do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or login credentials inside the app.

2. Local App Data

The app may store basic information locally on your device, such as reading progress, selected settings, subtitle preferences, and app preferences. This information stays on your device and is used only to improve your app experience.

3. Microphone Permission

Bayan Al-Qasas may request microphone access only when you choose to use reading or pronunciation practice features that involve recording your voice.

Audio recordings are intended for your own practice experience. The app does not upload your recordings to our servers.

4. Support Email

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, device details, app version, feedback, suggestions, or issue reports.

We use this information only to respond to your request and improve the app.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use standard platform services and Flutter packages to provide features such as audio playback, local storage, microphone recording, and opening email links. These services are used only to support the app’s features.

6. Children’s Privacy

Bayan Al-Qasas is designed as an educational app for Arabic learning. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can address the matter.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users’ personal information. We do not share personal information with advertisers.

8. Data Security

We aim to keep the app simple and privacy-friendly. Since most app data is stored locally on your device, you remain in control of your local app data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:

bayanalqasas.support@gmail.com
